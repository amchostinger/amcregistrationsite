WELCOME TO THE AFRICA METHODIST COUNCIL 3RD GENERAL CONFERENCE WEBSITE 

Since its inception in 1986, the Africa Methodist Council began to be fully active in 2015 with the Heads of Conference Summit held in Kumasi in Ghana. The first General Conference was held in 2019 in Kenya. The second General Conference was held in Cotonou, in Benin in March 2023. Africa Methodist Council Conferences are times for fellowship, spiritual enrichment, theological engagements, worship, learning from each other through workshops and seminars, community outreach, excursion, decision-making, appointing of leaders of the Council, celebrating special events, mission and vision casting, networking, collaborating, and communicating.  
The Third General Conference will be held from Tuesday 9 to Monday 15 March 2027 at Rainbow Towers, in Harare, Zimbabwe. Rainbow Towers is a 5 Star Hotel located 14 km/ 23 -30 minutes from Robert Mugabe International Airport and will be hosted by the Methodist Church in Zimbabwe.

Programme
The official programme starts at 0830Hrs on Wednesday March 10, 2026. 
There will be plenary and separate sessions during the Conference. Plenary Session are for all the delegates and separate session will be divided into Business Meetings of the delegates and presentations will continue for other delegates. 
We will also have time to celebrate the International Women’s Day on Thursday and all delegates will be wearing BLACK.
On Friday we will celebrate our diversity and all delegates will be wearing their church material/ logo
On Saturday delegates will celebrate the diversity of African culture and delegates will the traditional attire of their country. Delegates should also come for an excursion on Saturday after lunch. 
On Sunday, delegates will wear their Church uniforms for Sunday services around Harare. There wont be any official programme after Sunday services.
